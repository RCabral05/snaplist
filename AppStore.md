## Description (4,000 characters max)
Snaplist keeps your receipts, bills, statements and documents in one place and actually reads them, on your iPhone, with nothing uploaded.

SCAN OR IMPORT ANYTHING
• Scan paper receipts and letters with the camera, or add photos, PDFs and your bank's CSV exports
• Snaplist works out what each one is, who it's from, the date and the total
• Search every word on every page, including photos of labels and screenshots

ASK IN PLAIN WORDS
• "How much did I spend on takeout over the summer?"
• "When does my passport expire?"
• "What did I pay for eggs last time?"
• "Where's the spare HDMI cable?"
Answers are worked out from your own records, with the receipt or statement line one tap away. A purchase on both a receipt and a statement is counted once.

NEVER MISS A DATE
• Bills due, return windows closing, warranties ending, IDs and policies to renew
• Reminders days or weeks ahead, scheduled on your iPhone

SNAPLIST PRO
• Spending by month and category, budgets, and a recap on the 1st
• Price changes: your phone bill went up $24, Netflix went up $2.50
• Statement check: charges with no receipt, returns never credited, new subscriptions
• Live charges: log Apple Pay taps as you pay, or connect banks and cards through SimpleFIN
• Subscriptions: what everything that repeats costs a year, and a heads-up before trials charge
• Car: service history from receipts and when the next oil change is due
• Things: what you own with its receipt, warranty and manual, ready for an insurance claim
• Tax report for your accountant, grouped by Business, Medical, Charity and more
• Share a copy with card numbers, addresses and names blacked out
• Unlimited records and collections, and every theme

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no servers, no ads, no tracking
• Text is read and questions understood on the iPhone itself
• Optional Face ID lock
• Export or delete everything anytime

Free keeps up to 5 records. Snaplist Pro is $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial on the yearly plan. Payment is charged to your Apple Account and renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

## App Review notes
Snaplist has no account or sign-in. To try it, scan any receipt or add a photo of one
from the Add (+) tab, then ask a question on the Ask tab such as "how much did I spend
this month". Free allows 5 records; Snaplist Pro (auto-renewable subscription, monthly
or yearly) unlocks Spending, live charges, subscriptions, car, things, the tax report,
private-details sharing, unlimited records and collections, and themes. Pro can be bought
in the sandbox from Settings → Snaplist Pro. All processing is on device; there is no
server.

Live charges (Settings → Live charges) are optional: Apple Pay taps are logged by a
Shortcuts automation the person sets up, using the app's "Log a Charge" action, and banks
are read through SimpleFIN Bridge (simplefin.org), a read-only service the person signs up
for themselves and connects by pasting a setup token. Neither is needed to review the rest
of the app.
