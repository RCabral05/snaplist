Snaplist

Your receipts, bills, statements and documents, read and understood on your iPhone.

Scan or import paperwork and Snaplist reads it: what it is, who it's from, the date and the total. Then ask in plain words, like "how much did I spend on gas last month?" or "where's my Wi-Fi password?", and get an exact answer worked out on your iPhone, with the original one tap away.

No account. Nothing uploaded. Your archive stays on your iPhone.
