Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

In short: Snaplist has no account and no servers. Everything you add stays on your iPhone. We don't collect, see, sell or share your data, and there are no ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

The photos, PDFs, files and voice notes you add, the text Snaplist reads from them, the amounts, dates and names it finds, and everything you type or set in the app are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage, encrypted by iOS while your iPhone is locked. Reading text, recognizing speech and understanding questions all happen on the device. We, the developer, have no way to see any of it.

Your archive is included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer), which belong to your Apple Account and are covered by Apple's privacy policy. You can export everything or delete everything at any time in Settings.

Features that use another service, only if you turn them on

  • Name places from photos. When on, the location saved in a photo you add is sent to Apple's map service to find the town's name. The photo itself is not sent.
  • SimpleFIN Bridge. If you connect banks through SimpleFIN, Snaplist downloads your posted transactions from SimpleFIN using a connection you set up on SimpleFIN's site. Snaplist only downloads; it sends nothing about you back, and can't move money. The connection is kept in your iPhone's Keychain. SimpleFIN and the bank-data company it uses handle your bank data under their own privacy policies.
  • Apple Card. If you connect Apple Card, Snaplist reads its transactions from Wallet on your iPhone through Apple's FinanceKit. Nothing leaves the device.
  • Apple Pay taps. If you set up the Shortcuts automation, Wallet hands the merchant, amount and card name to Snaplist on your iPhone.
  • Apple Intelligence. When available and on, questions Snaplist's own rules don't follow are interpreted by Apple's on-device model.
  • Spotlight. If you turn it on, record names, dates and totals (never the text on a page) can appear in iPhone search.
  • Reminders. Notifications are scheduled on your iPhone. With the app lock on, amounts aren't shown on the Lock Screen.

Purchases

Snaplist Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment and tells the app only whether you have an active subscription. We never see your payment details.

Children

Snaplist isn't directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
