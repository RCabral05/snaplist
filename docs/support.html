Support

Help with Snaplist.

Contact: email support@example.com. Please don't send copies of your documents; describe what happened instead.

A total or date was read wrong

Open the record and tap the amount to correct it. Corrections are kept even if the record is read again. If a whole kind of receipt reads wrong, let us know what the store is and how the total is printed.

Something counted twice

When a receipt and a statement line look like the same purchase, Snaplist counts it once and shows the pair under "Counted once". Tap "Not the same" if they're different purchases.

Snaplist Pro

Restore a purchase in Settings → Snaplist Pro → Restore Purchases. Manage or cancel the subscription in the iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Free keeps up to 25 records; everything you've saved can always be opened, exported and deleted.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your archive comes along in an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone. You can also export everything as a zip in Settings → Your data.

Deleting your data

Settings → Your data → Delete Everything removes all records and originals from your iPhone. Deleting the app does the same. There's no copy anywhere else.
